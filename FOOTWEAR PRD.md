# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Title

Priya Foot Wear – Men's Footwear Product Catalog Website

## Version

1.0

## Product Owner

Priya Foot Wear

## Overview

Priya Foot Wear requires a modern, mobile-friendly website that showcases its men's footwear collection online. The website will function as a digital catalog rather than an e-commerce platform.

Customers will be able to browse products, view details, and contact the store via WhatsApp or phone to make purchases.

No online payments, shopping cart, checkout, or user accounts will be included.

---

# Business Goals

### Primary Goals

* Increase local brand visibility.
* Showcase available footwear collections online.
* Generate customer inquiries through WhatsApp and phone.
* Improve customer trust and store credibility.
* Create a digital presence for future expansion.

### Success Metrics

* Number of product inquiries via WhatsApp.
* Number of contact form submissions.
* Website traffic growth.
* Product page views.
* Customer visits generated from website.

---

# Target Audience

## Primary Audience

Men aged 18–55 looking for:

* Formal shoes
* Casual shoes
